# Analysis status

The reproducible analysis workflow now regenerates 2D/3D stress images, creep margins and mesh comparisons. See [the current guide](../../README.md), [results](RESULTS.md) and [run status](https://github.com/thereprocase/spool-wall-rack/actions/workflows/engineering-analysis.yml).

Current printable CAD remains level-rail E8. The +12 mm front rail is a separate statics study. Serviceability limits are 5 mm total loaded movement and 1 mm per-roll change. These supersede earlier provisional movement limits.
