# E + F direct-brace body

Implemented CAD: a six-millimetre-wide diagonal connects the lower mounting region directly to the keel. The upper mast retains the six-millimetre web and tapered shoulders from ef-cut-v4. Protected G bearing/capture geometry has zero symmetric difference; fixed mounting and moulding checks pass.

The original two-wall, 0.6 mm skin schedule in selected-layout.json was not sliced. Subsequent implemented ASA schedules retain this body and use 1.6 mm skins. They provide aligned body/helper STEP files, model-only 3MF, actual slices and verification.
