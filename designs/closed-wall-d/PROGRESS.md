# Closed-wall bracket — revision D progress

This checkpoint is the closed-wall concept only. The open-web reference remains a separate future exploration.

The bracket is printed on its side. Two continuous 1.2 mm internal L-shaped plates and two 1.2 mm outside faces enclose three cavities stacked across the 24 mm width. The plate planes are parallel to the print bed and to the principal installed bending load. The exploded rendering separates these planes for explanation; the actual bracket is a single part with a continuous perimeter.

Print assumptions: three 0.45 mm walls, 15% infill with zero structural credit, 1.2 mm top and bottom, 0.2 mm layers. The single-part envelope is approximately 243 x 240 x 24 mm. Pair-print requirement is removed. Nominal 25.4 mm wooden dowels use 26 mm seats on 150 mm centers.

These are renders from the CAD mesh, with illustrative dowels. They are progress views, not a strength or printability certification. Internal roof bridging, slicer behavior, fastener installation, and material-specific long-term creep limits still require validation. No maximum spool rating is established.
