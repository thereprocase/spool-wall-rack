# Rev G wider inner-seat support — rejected local perturbation

This variant increases only the inner-seat dense support from 5 to 8 mm,
starting from the full-plane feedback finalist. The body STEP/STLs are
identical; the modifier and actual printed material change.

It slices to **108.03 g**, 4.27 g above the parent, and passes the nominal
toolpath checks. Coarse h2 front movement improves to 2.988 mm, but raw
tensile stress remains 21.25 MPa, giving a 1.91 coupon-screen ratio against
the requested four. No refined acceptance or genetic optimality is claimed.
